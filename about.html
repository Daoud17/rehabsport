About Rehab Sports Center

Physiotherapy, Sports Injury Rehabilitation & Recovery — Sin El Fil, Lebanon.

Our Philosophy

At Rehab Sports Center, we believe physiotherapy is about more than relieving pain. It's about restoring movement, rebuilding strength, and helping every patient return to the activities they enjoy with confidence.

Our approach combines personalized rehabilitation, functional assessment, therapeutic exercises, and progressive recovery plans. Whether you're an athlete recovering from a sports injury, a football player preparing to return to the field, or someone looking to improve everyday mobility, our focus is on safe, effective, and lasting recovery.

We believe every recovery journey is different. That's why rehabilitation should be guided by individual goals, regular progress assessments, and a structured approach to movement and physical performance.

Our Expertise & Focus

  • Physiotherapy and musculoskeletal rehabilitation
  • Sports injury rehabilitation and injury prevention
  • Football injury rehabilitation and return-to-play preparation
  • ACL, knee, ankle, and shoulder rehabilitation
  • Post-operative physiotherapy and functional recovery
  • Strength, mobility, balance, and movement training
  • Personalized rehabilitation for athletes and everyday patients

Experience & Approach

A structured approach to physiotherapy, sports injury rehabilitation, and long-term recovery.

Our Rehabilitation Approach

01 — ASSESSMENT

Understanding your injury, symptoms, movement limitations, and personal recovery goals through an individual assessment.

02 — TREATMENT

Developing a personalized physiotherapy plan using appropriate rehabilitation techniques, therapeutic exercises, and movement-based interventions.

03 — REHABILITATION

Progressively restoring mobility, strength, balance, and functional movement to support recovery and physical performance.

04 — RETURN TO ACTIVITY

Supporting a safe return to everyday activities, exercise, and sports, including football, through progressive rehabilitation and readiness assessment.
