OUR REHABILITATION PROGRAMS

Programs Built Around Your Recovery

Personalized physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation programs designed to restore movement, rebuild strength, and support a safe return to everyday activities and sports.

Joint Rehabilitation Program

One joint. One recovery plan.

A structured rehabilitation program for knee, ankle, shoulder, and hip conditions, focusing on pain reduction, mobility, stability, and progressive strengthening.

What the program includes

  • Joint mobility and flexibility exercises
  • Progressive muscle strengthening
  • Balance and stability training
  • Functional movement rehabilitation
  • Regular progress assessments

Return-to-Sport Program

Recover stronger. Return with confidence.

A progressive sports rehabilitation pathway helping athletes rebuild strength, agility, coordination, and sport-specific movement after injury.

What the program includes

  • Strength and functional movement assessments
  • Agility and change-of-direction training
  • Jumping and landing mechanics
  • Sport-specific rehabilitation exercises
  • Return-to-sport readiness evaluation

Sports Performance Assessment

Understand your movement. Improve your performance.

A structured assessment of strength, balance, flexibility, and movement patterns to identify physical limitations and establish a performance baseline.

What the program includes

  • Muscle strength assessment
  • Range-of-motion evaluation
  • Balance and coordination testing
  • Functional movement screening
  • Individualized exercise recommendations

ACL & Knee Rehabilitation

Rebuild stability. Restore confidence.

A phased rehabilitation program for ACL injuries and knee surgery recovery, progressing from early mobility to strengthening and functional activity.

What the program includes

  • Early-stage mobility rehabilitation
  • Quadriceps and hamstring strengthening
  • Knee stability and balance exercises
  • Running and agility progression
  • Functional testing before sports participation

Football Injury Rehabilitation

From the sidelines back to the pitch.

Sports physiotherapy focused on common football injuries, including ankle sprains, hamstring strains, knee injuries, and muscle overload.

What the program includes

  • Football injury assessment
  • Targeted muscle rehabilitation
  • Running and sprinting progression
  • Cutting and directional-change exercises
  • Football-specific movement training

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Guided recovery at every stage.

Individualized physiotherapy following orthopedic procedures, helping patients gradually restore mobility, muscle strength, and daily function.

What the program includes

  • Post-operative mobility exercises
  • Swelling and stiffness management
  • Progressive muscle strengthening
  • Walking and movement retraining
  • Functional recovery monitoring

Injury Prevention & Conditioning

Train smarter. Move better.

A preventive physiotherapy program addressing strength deficits, movement limitations, and physical conditioning needs in active individuals.

What the program includes

  • Movement and posture assessment
  • Muscle imbalance identification
  • Core and lower-limb strengthening
  • Flexibility and mobility training
  • Sport-specific injury prevention exercises

Back & Spine Rehabilitation

Better movement. Stronger support.

Physiotherapy for back pain, spinal mobility limitations, and muscular discomfort, focusing on movement control, strength, and daily function.

What the program includes

  • Spinal mobility exercises
  • Core stability training
  • Postural and movement education
  • Progressive functional strengthening
  • Individualized home exercise guidance
